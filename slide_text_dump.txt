Abstract | ATSForge is a unified web platform for resume building, ATS readiness scoring, JD matching, cover-letter generation, and professional outreach. It uses a five-factor hybrid scoring model to improve resume quality before job applications are submitted.

--- ppt\slides\slide4.xml ---
What is ATSForge? | Introduction | Modern hiring workflows depend on Applicant Tracking Systems and role-specific screening. | Students often use different tools for resume writing, keyword checking, JD matching, and outreach. | ATSForge combines these tasks into one AI-assisted workflow built on a shared resume schema.

--- ppt\slides\slide5.xml ---
Why is this project required? | Problem | Many resumes fail because of weak formatting, missing keywords, poor evidence, or low relevance to the target role. | Most public tools return a single opaque score and do not connect ATS feedback with JD alignment or document generation. | Applicants need transparent, actionable guidance inside one end-to-end system.

--- ppt\slides\slide6.xml ---
Objectives | What does it do? | Build and parse resumes using a structured candidate profile. | Score resumes with transparent factor-level ATS feedback. | Analyze JD alignment and generate tailored cover letters and outreach drafts. | Support export-ready workflows through preview and formatting tools.

--- ppt\slides\slide7.xml ---
Innovation | Shared Resume Schema + Hybrid ATS Scoring + JD-Aware AI Content | Qualitative: Transparent factor-level feedback instead of one opaque keyword score. | Quantitative: ATS = 0.22SC + 0.26AE + 0.18KS + 0.18PS + 0.16LQ.

--- ppt\slides\slide13.xml ---
Result | ATSForge integrates resume building, ATS scoring, JD matching, cover-letter writing, and outreach generation in one platform. | The five-factor hybrid model provides interpretable feedback on structure, evidence, keywords, parse safety, and language quality. | Internal validation showed better workflow reuse, faster document preparation, and clearer improvement guidance for users.

--- ppt\slides\slide14.xml ---
Conclusion | ATSForge demonstrates that resume optimization, role matching, and application drafting can be combined into one AI-assisted academic product. | Its hybrid scoring model is transparent, modular, and suitable for further refinement. | Future scope includes recruiter-side validation, multilingual support, and larger cross-industry benchmarking.
